# Saathi — User Guide

## Part A · For seniors (print this page in large type)

### The screen

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 🏠 Home  ⬅️ Back  🔁 Repeat  🙋 HELP │  ← always here
├──────────────────────────────────────┤
│  A−  A+   🌙 Dark   🗣️ Voice   🌐   │  ← make it easier to see / hear
├──────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                      │
│        Saathi asks a question        │
│        and shows BIG buttons         │
│                                      │
├──────────────────────────────────────┤
│        🎤  Tap to speak              │  ← press and talk
└──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Signing in
1. Open Saathi → tap **"I am a Senior Citizen"**.
2. Type your **mobile number** on the big keypad, then your **4-digit PIN**. It signs you in automatically.
3. Saathi opens in your language. Change it anytime with 🌐.
4. To sign out, tap **🚪 Logout**.

### How to use
1. **Listen.** Saathi speaks every question aloud.
2. **Answer**: tap **🎤 Tap to speak**, then talk (the button turns red while listening; tap again to stop) **or** tap a big button.
3. Missed it? Press **🔁 Repeat** or say *"repeat" / "फिर से" / "మళ్ళీ"*.
4. Wrong choice? Press **⬅️ Back** or say *"back" / "पीछे" / "వెనక్కి"*.
5. Confused? Press the orange **🙋 HELP**. A volunteer will see where you are and send you a message, which Saathi reads aloud.

### Hearing the options your way
When Saathi shows several options (cities, trains, contacts…), tap 🎤 and say:

| You want to… | Say (English · हिन्दी · తెలుగు) |
|---|---|
| hear **only one** option | "read option 2" · "दूसरा विकल्प बताओ" · "రెండో ఎంపిక చెప్పండి" |
| hear them **one at a time** | "one by one" · "एक-एक करके" · "ఒక్కొక్కటిగా" — then "yes" to choose or "next" |
| find the **cheapest / fastest** | "the cheapest" · "सबसे सस्ता" · "తక్కువ ధర" |
| pick by **time** | "the morning train" · "शाम वाली" · "రాత్రి బస్సు" |

Or tap the small **🔊** on any option to hear just that one. If Saathi is not sure what you said, it asks *"Did you mean …?"* first.

### Paying a bill — what to expect
1. Say *"electricity bill"* / *"बिजली का बिल"* / *"కరెంట్ బిల్లు"*.
2. Saathi reads your number back — say **Yes** only if it is right.
3. Saathi tells you the amount. Say **Yes** to pay.
4. A **"Please check carefully"** screen shows who you are paying and how much.
5. **Press and HOLD** the green button until it fills up. (A quick touch does nothing — so you can't pay by mistake.)
6. You get **5 seconds** to press **CANCEL** if you change your mind.
7. You hear "Done!" and a receipt number.

### Bigger payments need your PIN
For payments of ₹1,000 or more, Saathi asks for your 4-digit PIN after you hold the green button. **Type it — never say it aloud.** After 3 wrong PINs the payment is stopped to protect your money. If you make 5 wrong PINs while signing in, Saathi locks for 15 minutes.

### Reading a bill or letter
Tap **📄 Read a bill / letter** (or say *"read my bill"*, *"बिल पढ़कर सुनाओ"*, *"బిల్లు చదివి వినిపించు"*).
1. Tap **📷 Take a photo** (hold the paper flat, in good light) or **📁 Choose a file** (PDF). You can also paste the text.
2. Saathi tells you who sent it, how much to pay, the last date and how many days are left.
3. If it is a bill Saathi can pay, tap **Pay this bill** — the number is filled in for you, and you still confirm everything.
4. If Saathi says it looks like a scam — **do not pay, do not call the number on it.** Show it to family.

### Checking your past payments
Tap **🕘 History** at the top (or say *"show my payments"*). You will see:
- ✅ **Paid** — the money went.
- ❌ **Failed** — Saathi stopped it to keep you safe (too much for one day, or the PIN was wrong 3 times). No money was taken.
- ⏸ **Stopped** — you cancelled it, or left before saying yes. No money was taken. Tap **Try again** to pay that bill again.
- 🕒 **Left half-way** — things you started but did not finish, and the step where you stopped.
Say *"failed"*, *"stopped"* or *"paid"* to see only those.

### When you need a person
Tap **🆘 Help** (or say *"help"*, or press SOS on the remote). Choose:
- **📞 Call my son / daughter** — Saathi shows who and the number first, then you pick phone call or video call.
- **🤝 Ask a volunteer** — a volunteer sees where you are and sends a hint, which Saathi reads aloud.
- **🚨 I think someone is cheating me** — follow the 4 steps on screen. Tap **Call 1930** for the national cyber-crime helpline.

### Stay safe
- 🔒 **Saathi will NEVER ask for your OTP or PIN.** Nobody genuine will either.
- Got a strange SMS? Tap **🛡️ Check a Message** and read it aloud. Red screen = scam.
- If a payment is very large, Saathi stops it and asks you to get family approval.

### The help remote (optional)
| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🔵 SPEAK | same as the microphone button |
| 🟢 YES | answers yes · also completes the "hold to pay" step |
| 🔴 NO | answers no · cancels a payment |
| 🟠 SOS | calls a volunteer |

It buzzes when you press a button so you know it worked.

## Part B · For volunteers

The **📜 Payment & session log** at the bottom of the dashboard shows every senior's payments (paid / failed / stopped, with the reason and the step) and the tasks they left half-way. Pick one senior from the list to see only theirs.


Open Saathi → **"I am a Volunteer"** → sign in with your email and password. The dashboard refreshes every 5 seconds. Use **Logout** at the top right when done.

| Section | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Needs help now** | Someone pressed HELP. You can see their task and step. Type a short hint (or click a quick hint) → **Send hint** — it is spoken on their phone. Click **Mark resolved** when done. |
| **Struggling right now** | People in a task in the last 30 minutes. ⚠ *Stuck* = 3+ failed voice tries on one step — consider calling them. |
| **Task funnel** | How many people reached each step. The red note marks the biggest drop → that step needs a simpler design. |
| **Trouble spots** | Steps ranked by people giving up and by voice retries. |
| **Voice recognition by language** | Failures per session for each language. |
| **Phrases not understood** | Exactly what the system heard. Ask the developer to add these words to the vocabulary. |

The first time you run the app the dashboard shows **sample demo activity** — press **Clear demo data** before real use.

## Part C · For testers (speech accuracy)

1. Open **`/speech-test`**. Fill in tester code, age group, language, accent/region and noise level.
2. Measure noise with a phone sound-meter app and pick the matching level.
3. Press **Start recording**, read the sentence on screen, wait. The result (word accuracy, character accuracy, intent correct?) appears and the next sentence loads.
4. Repeat for each tester and condition. The bottom table groups results by language × accent × noise.
5. **Export CSV** and paste the table into `docs/TEST_REPORT.md`.
